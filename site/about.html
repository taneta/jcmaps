About JC Maps

What's on in Jersey City (today, tomorrow or this weekend) on one map. Free to use and non-commercial: no ads, no sign-up.

Where the events come from

Know a calendar, website or newsletter that lists events? That's the most helpful suggestion, because one source can bring dozens of events. Connecting a new source takes some work, so give it a few weeks.

Suggest a source ↗

JC Maps collects events only from sources that share them openly, such as public calendars, and treats them with respect: it keeps the facts (what, when and where), sums each event up in its own words, and always links back to the original listing. The sources so far:

Who makes it

Hi, I'm Katerina, and I've lived in Jersey City since 2020. A lot happens in this city: story times, free concerts, park cleanups, meetups. I'm always wondering where to take my kids or where to hang out with friends, and the answer is usually spread across calendars and chats. Let's bring them together on one map!

I also want to move civic tech and community projects in Jersey City forward: small, open tools that make public information easy to use and bring neighbors together. If you're working on a community or civic tech project here, or have an idea for one, tell me about it: send me a message ↗.
